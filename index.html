Skills: Insight. Later, take Expertise in Stealth and Sleight of Hand.

Combat loop

  1. Start fights from stealth to trigger Surprise.
  2. Shadow Step for Advantage, then use Sneak Attack.
  3. Use Stunning Strike on threats. Hide or teleport out.
